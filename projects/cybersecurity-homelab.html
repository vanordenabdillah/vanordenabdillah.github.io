pfSense Nessus Wazuh Metasploitable Cybersecurity / infrastructure

A virtualized home lab used to practice offensive and defensive security: a segmented network behind a pfSense firewall, with Metasploitable VMs as intentionally vulnerable targets, scanned with Nessus and monitored with Wazuh.

Overview

The lab is a set of virtual machines networked together to mimic a small, intentionally-vulnerable environment: a pfSense box acting as the router/firewall at the network edge, one or more Metasploitable VMs as deliberately exploitable targets, a Nessus instance for vulnerability scanning, and Wazuh as a SIEM for log collection and alerting. The goal was to get hands-on experience with the full loop - scanning for vulnerabilities, understanding what they mean, and then seeing the resulting activity show up in detection tooling.

Lab environment

The lab is designed to simulate a small segmented environment, with pfSense sitting between the internal machines and the wider network so traffic can be controlled and logged.

Vulnerability scanning (Nessus)

Nessus was run against the Metasploitable VMs to enumerate known vulnerabilities - outdated services, weak configurations, and so on - and produce prioritized findings by severity.

Detection & SIEM (Wazuh)

Wazuh collects logs from across the lab and flags suspicious activity - including activity generated by the Nessus scans themselves and by exploitation attempts against the Metasploitable VMs - giving visibility into what an attack looks like from the defender's side.
