Compliance Review: Georgia Tech Website Privacy Policies

Paper / write-up

A review of Georgia Tech's website privacy policies, checking stated data practices against relevant privacy regulations and institutional obligations.

Overview

This paper reviews the privacy policy and data-handling disclosures published on Georgia Tech's website against a set of applicable compliance frameworks, such as FERPA (for student-record-adjacent data), general state/federal privacy expectations for public institutions, EU GDPR, PRC PIPL, and common web-privacy best practices around cookies, analytics, and third-party trackers.

The review analyzes the Georgia Tech Privacy Policy, Data Goveranance Policy, Security Camera Use Policy, Privacy and Legal Notice, and identifes privacy risks and governance gaps. It then goes on to give policy recommendations and outlines a possible Surveillance Privacy Policy.
